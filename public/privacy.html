Privacy at Kornu

Kornu lets you save articles, read them, and keep your own notes. This page explains what happens to the information you choose to put there.

Updated 16 November 2026

What we handle

When you sign in with Google, Kornu receives your account identifier, email address, and basic profile information needed to identify your library. Google handles your sign-in credentials; Kornu does not receive your Google password.

When you save a reading, Kornu handles its original URL, extracted article text and supported images. We store the highlights, notes, and reading progress you add. Your browser may keep local copies so your library can load and retry a save if syncing pauses.

The Kornu Clipper reads the current tab's URL and title when you open it. When you choose to save, it sends the URL to Kornu. It stores your chosen Kornu site address in Chrome's extension storage. If you choose a different site address, Chrome may ask you to allow the extension to read that site's save status.

How we use it

We use this information to create your account, capture the readings you choose, show your library and annotations, and keep them with your profile. Kornu does not use your saved content for advertising or sell it.

Capturing an article requires Kornu's server to request the original article and supported image URLs. Some publishers block these requests, so a reading may need review or remain a link to the original page.

Services involved

Google provides sign-in. Supabase provides account, database, and private file storage. Netlify hosts the web app and processes article capture requests. These services process information needed to operate Kornu under their own service terms and privacy practices. A source website receives a request when Kornu tries to capture its article or images.

How long it stays

Your account and saved content stay in Kornu while your account is active, unless you ask us to remove them. Browser copies remain until you clear Kornu's site data. Hosting and storage providers may retain operational records according to their own policies.

Control and deletion

You can export a backup from Kornu's profile menu. Signing out does not automatically erase cached readings from that browser; clearing Kornu's site data removes those local copies. Removing the extension does not delete your Kornu account or saved readings.

To request deletion of your account and stored readings, contact us at reyhantamang@gmail.com. We will confirm the request and remove associated saved files in private storage as part of the deletion.

Questions and changes

For privacy questions, contact reyhantamang@gmail.com. If Kornu's data handling changes, we will update this page and its date.
