The art of paying attention

Kornu sample8 min read

There is a kind of attention that asks nothing of us except that we stay a little longer.

We look again at the room, the street, the familiar face, and something ordinary begins to acquire its own shape.

A notebook can hold these moments before they disappear. When we return, the page carries the first reading alongside the second.
